FAQ

Common questions

Quick answers about how escrow works. Can't find what you need? Ask our team.

What is escrow?

Escrow is an arrangement where an independent third party holds funds or documents on behalf of the parties to a transaction, and releases them only when agreed conditions have been met.

Who decides when funds are released?

Release conditions are set out in the escrow agreement signed by all parties before any deposit is made. We release strictly in line with that agreement.

What do you need to get started?

We need details of the transaction and the parties involved. As part of onboarding we carry out identity and source-of-funds checks on every engagement.

How are your fees calculated?

Fees depend on the size and complexity of the transaction. We provide a clear quote before you commit to anything.

What happens if there is a dispute?

The escrow agreement sets out how disputes are handled. Funds or documents remain held until the matter is resolved in the way the agreement provides.
