The Process

How escrow works

A clear, structured process so every party knows exactly what happens, and when.

01

Agree the Terms

All parties agree the transaction conditions and sign the escrow agreement.

02

Deposit

Funds or documents are placed into escrow and receipt is confirmed to each party.

03

Fulfil Conditions

Each party completes their obligations and the agreed conditions are verified.

04

Release

Once conditions are satisfied, funds or documents are released as instructed.
