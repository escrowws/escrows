Who We Are

Built around trust and clarity

Maslom International Pty Ltd provides escrow services for businesses, buyers, sellers, and transaction partners. Our role is simple: to hold funds and documents securely, and to release them only when the conditions everyone agreed to have been met.

Every engagement is governed by a written escrow agreement, so each party understands exactly what will happen, when, and on whose instruction.

  • Independent and neutral between all parties
  • Clear written release conditions for every transaction
  • Transparent communication at each stage
  • Identity and source-of-funds checks on every engagement
Neutral by designWe follow the agreement, never the interests of one side.
